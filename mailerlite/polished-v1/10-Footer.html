Pay It Forward

You don't have to have the words. You just have to be the one who passes it on.

Share It Directly

Send a link to the digital book or audiobook to the person you thought of. A text that says "this made me think of you" is enough. BookFunnel makes the delivery seamless — they'll get their own copy, no cost to them, no hoops.

Fund a Copy for Someone You May Never Meet

Your contribution puts Disappointing Grace in the hands of people who need it — through churches, recovery ministries, support groups, and chaplains working with people in crisis. Every dollar goes directly to producing and delivering copies. No overhead, no middleman.

For Churches & Ministries

If you lead a small group, recovery ministry, or pastoral care team and want to make the book available to the people you serve — reach out. We'll work with you on bulk copies, group pricing, or partnership.
