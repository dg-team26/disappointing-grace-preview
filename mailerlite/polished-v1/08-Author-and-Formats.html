About the Author

Dave Fleet

Dave built a multimillion-dollar company, lost it all, nearly quit — but then found God in the ashes. He was put back together by Jesus, a faithful wife, and people who refused to let him stay broken.

Now he spends his life helping others find their way back. He wrote Disappointing Grace because he believes there are a lot of people hurting quietly who've drifted from God and just need someone to say, "me too." The audiobook is read in his own voice, because some things land better heard than read.

"If my story helps even one person stop hiding and start healing — it was worth telling."
