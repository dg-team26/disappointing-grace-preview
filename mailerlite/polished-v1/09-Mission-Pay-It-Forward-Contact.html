The Mission

Help broken people heal, and point them back to Jesus.

That's the whole thing. Dave has plenty of options for what to do with this stretch of his life. He chose this one — because too many people are hurting quietly, and too many have drifted from God and from the kind of community that could walk with them.

The book is a bridge, not the destination. If it helps even one person take a step back toward honesty, toward hope, toward Jesus and a real church — it was worth writing.

Pay It Forward

A name probably came to mind.

If this book meant something to you, there's a good chance you thought of someone while you read it — someone in a hard season, someone praying into silence right now. The most valuable thing you can do isn't for Dave. It's to put the book in their hands.

A copy, an audio link, a text that says "this made me think of you." You don't have to have the words. You just have to be the one who passes it on.
